# GUGK Atlas 1990 research extraction

Temporary research raster extracted from the image-container scan of **Атлас мира (1990)**, produced by ПКО «Картография» ГУГК СССР, prepared in 1989 and corrected in 1990.

- `page-03-world.jpg`: political world map immediately after contents.
- `page-04-ussr.jpg`: full USSR map at 1:40,000,000, immediately before the regional USSR sheets.
- Scan host: Internet Archive item `antonov_vm_red_atlas_mira_1990`.
- Canonical provenance must cite the original GUGK atlas, not Internet Archive as map author.

These rasters are research inputs only; they are not themselves History Core geometry.
